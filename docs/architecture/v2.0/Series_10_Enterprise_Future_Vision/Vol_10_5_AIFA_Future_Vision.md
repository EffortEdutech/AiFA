## 1. Purpose

This closing volume restates AIFA's long-term direction in light of the complete Version 2.0 architecture, without introducing new binding decisions — it is a synthesis, not a new ADR.

## 2. The Unchanged Core

> One Input. AI Does the Rest.

Every volume in this ten-series set exists to make this promise true, safely, at increasing scale — from a single SME owner recording one receipt, to an enterprise group with multiple entities and industries.

## 3. Long-Term Direction

- **Deeper local intelligence**, reducing cloud dependency for the core loop over time (Vol 10_3).
- **Broader industry coverage**, through composable Industry Finance PKA extensions rather than product forks (Vol 10_2).
- **Ecosystem participation**, benefiting from Knowledge Factory improvements shared across runtimes (Vol 10_4).
- **Enterprise-grade scale**, without weakening any governance boundary that protects individual SME owners today (Vol 10_0, Vol 10_1).
- **A growing extension ecosystem**, letting long-tail business needs be met without bloating the core product (Series 9).

## 4. What AIFA Is, Restated

AIFA is not merely a bookkeeping application. It is a local-first, PKA-powered, AI-assisted financial intelligence platform for SMEs, designed to make professional financial capability accessible through natural business interaction while preserving governance, explainability, ownership, and long-term organisational knowledge.

## 5. Standing Commitments Carried Forward

1. Business Events remain the canonical source of truth (ADR-001).
2. The AI never sees the full Finance PKA or full business database — only a minimal, governed PCB.
3. Professional knowledge is governed by Knowledge Factory; business knowledge is owned by the business.
4. Irreversible actions require human approval.
5. The architecture remains technology-neutral at this documentation layer; implementation specifics belong to a future Series 11.

## 6. Relationships to Other Volumes

This volume synthesises Vol 1_0 (Product Vision), Vol 0_0 (Master Documentation Index), and Vol 10_0–10_4 (Enterprise & Future Vision). It introduces no new architecture and should be read as the closing statement of the Version 2.0 set.
