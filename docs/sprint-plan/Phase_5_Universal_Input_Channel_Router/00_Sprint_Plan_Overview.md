## Why this phase exists

The owner's stated goal is Vol 10_5 §2's founding principle, "One Input. AI Does the Rest.," made concrete for actual day-to-day solopreneur use. Vol_5_5 originally audited this against Phases 1-4's three worked examples (receipt, leave application, purchase order) and designed a router scoped to close exactly those three gaps.

**Scope revision, 14 September 2026 — the owner's actual objective is broader than three examples.** In the owner's own words: *"AiFA will serve company owners with minimum employee to handle business operation. 1 input, AiFA do the rest,"* named explicitly across every module AiFA ships — Sales (Parties, Pricing & Catalog, Quotations, Invoices, Payments & Credit Notes, AR Ageing), Purchases & Cash (Payment Vouchers, Expense, Cash Book/P&L), Inventory (Products & Stock, Delivery Orders), Accounting (Chart of Accounts, Ledger, Full Reports & Bank Reconciliation), Compliance (e-Invoice & SST), People (Payroll, Attendance & Leave, Commission), and Legal (Contracts & Alerts, e-Signature) — with human approval in the loop and the workflow continuing automatically to completion once approved. Measured against this, the plan as it stood after Sprint 58 (Purchase Order) would have closed only 3 of these roughly 14 domains, and explicitly named "any domain beyond leave_application and purchase_order" as out of scope for the whole phase. That exclusion is removed by this revision; the "Sprint list" and "Explicitly out of scope" sections below reflect the corrected scope.

This revision does not discard any completed or in-flight work. It generalizes two patterns Phases 1 and 4 already proved individually, instead of inventing new mechanisms per domain:

1. **AI-backed classification already exists — it just isn't wired to Path B yet.** `packages/core/src/ai/capturePipeline.ts` already has a working, LLM-backed `classify()` with confidence scoring (`CategoryClassificationResult`, and an `AiLedgerDomain` type already covering `"expense" | "sale" | "purchase"`) — but it runs only against Path A (the local, end-to-end-encrypted tables no live web report reads from; see Sprint 53's own CORRECTION note). Sprints 53-56's router instead used `classifyChannelIntakeDomain`, a deliberate keyword/regex heuristic covering only `expense`/`leave_application`/`unclassified`, disclosed at the time as a starting point pending "a real AI-provider-backed router" (Sprint 53's own text, Vol_5_5 §7). Revised Sprint 57 is that disclosed upgrade: port the existing `classify()` pattern to Path B and widen its domain coverage to the full module list, retiring the regex heuristic rather than patching it domain by domain.
2. **Draft-then-approve-then-post already exists and is proven live.** `createPaymentVoucher`/`createCreditNote` (`packages/core/src/sync/paymentVouchersReportsTransport.ts`) already create a `draft`-status row, route it through the real `create_approval_task` engine, and only post to the ledger on a separate, explicit action (`markPaymentVoucherPaid`) once approved — exactly the "AI drafts, a human approves, the workflow continues to completion" shape the owner described. Sprint 58's own PO chaining rule ("approved step re-enters the router as a new intake," Vol_5_5 §9) is the second proven pattern. Revised Sprints 58-60 apply both patterns, module group by module group, instead of building a bespoke pipeline per domain; revised Sprint 61 generalizes the chaining rule and Sprint 57 (original)'s confidence/trust routing table across all of them at once, now that there is more than one domain-bridge sprint to route between.

## Sequencing principle

Sprint 53 (the generic in-app router + domain expansion) ships first, exactly as Sprint 37 anchored Phase 4 — every later sprint's new channel or domain feeds the same core this sprint re-establishes. Sprint 55 (media/voice foundation) was pulled forward for the reason recorded 6 September 2026: every later channel inherits one real extraction path instead of re-solving it per channel.

Sprint 54 and 56 add channels (web paste/drop, then mobile share-target) and can be reprioritised relative to each other; both depend on 53, and 56 additionally depends on 55's media-extraction pipeline for its image/PDF path.

**Revised sequencing from Sprint 57 onward (14 September 2026):** the classifier upgrade must come before any domain-bridge sprint can route a real AI-detected domain anywhere useful, so revised Sprint 57 (Universal AI Domain Classification) now sits directly after the channel sprints and before every domain-bridge sprint — a stronger dependency than the original Sprint 57 (confidence routing) had, because 58-60 now depend on 57 actually classifying their domains, not merely on a channel existing to route. The three domain-bridge sprints (58 Sales & Purchases, 59 People & Inventory, 60 Accounting/Compliance/Legal) are grouped by which existing Path B transports and approval shapes they reuse, not by business importance, and can in principle be resequenced relative to each other if the owner's actual usage pattern makes one group more urgent — each is independent of the other two once 57 is done. Sprint 61 (cross-domain chaining and confidence trust) depends on at least one domain-bridge sprint existing, exactly as the original Sprint 57 depended on at least one channel sprint existing. Sprint 62 closes the phase, mirroring Sprint 49's role in Phase 4.

## What "Definition of Done" means for a Phase 5 sprint (read once, applies to every sprint below)

Every Phase 5 sprint's DoD centres on: (1) the new channel or domain genuinely reaches the existing `classifyAndRoute`/Business Event core (from Sprint 57 onward: the ported Path B `classify()`, not a per-domain special case) — no parallel pipeline, per Vol_5_5 §2's own governing rule; (2) nothing from a new channel or domain auto-posts above what the confidence/trust routing (Sprint 57 original scope, generalized in revised Sprint 61) allows until that table says otherwise — concretely, every domain-bridge sprint (58-60) ships its domain as a **draft** requiring approval first, never a direct ledger post, exactly like `createPaymentVoucher` already does; (3) `npm run typecheck` and `npm run lint` pass clean in both `web/` and `app/`; (4) any genuine environment constraint this session's device-bridge shell cannot verify live is disclosed exactly as Sprint 37/40/49/53 disclosed such gaps — never silently assumed working; (5) any deliberately deferred scope is named as still-open, not silently dropped. A sprint's own DoD list states these five plus anything sprint-specific.

## Explicitly out of scope for this phase

- **A true unattended inbound listener** (a public WhatsApp Business number or receiving mailbox with no owner-forwarding step) — Vol_5_5 §11 names this a distinct, harder, deferred upgrade; not part of Phase 5.
- **A working voice-transcription backend** — Sprint 55 built the `AiProvider.transcribeAudio()` contract and the UI states, but no shipped provider implements it; still a real open item for the owner to resolve before voice notes work anywhere, unaffected by this revision.
- **The Gateway's own `/ai-vision` server-side route** — Sprint 55 documented and built the client-side contract against it; `ai-gateway-service` is a separate repository, and the route itself must be built there before web's image/PDF extraction works end-to-end.
- **Full feature depth within each newly-bridged domain** — revised Sprints 58-60 each ship ONE proven draft→approve→post (or chain) path per domain, matching Sprint 58 (original)'s own stated discipline of proving one PO chain rather than a fully-featured purchasing module. Partial-line PO receiving, PO amendment, multi-supplier consolidation, payroll-run automation beyond a single commission draft, and e-Invoice/SST auto-filing (as opposed to flagging for review) are named explicitly as open items at each sprint's own close, not silently built in.
- **~~Any domain beyond leave_application and purchase_order~~ — removed by this revision.** This was the exclusion the owner's 14 September 2026 objective statement showed to be too narrow; the corrected scope is the Sprint list above.
